✏️ 12 Animation Principles

  • Squash & Stretch: Show weight & flexibility
  • Anticipation: Prepare viewer for action
  • Staging: Present idea clearly
  • Straight Ahead / Pose-to-Pose: Two drawing methods
  • Follow Through & Overlapping: Parts keep moving
  • Slow In & Slow Out: More frames at start & end
  • Arcs: Natural movement follows curves
  • Secondary Action: Supporting movements
  • Timing: Frames determine speed
  • Exaggeration: Emphasize for impact
  • Solid Drawing: Give volume & dimension
  • Appeal: Engaging design & personality
